# Presentation Strategy

## Audience

Agricultural business stakeholders and other non-technical users.

## Story

Use the sequence:

**Problem → Data → Pattern → Prediction → Reliability → Action**

## Recommended visuals

- Simple trend charts
- Crop/region comparison charts
- Actual-vs-predicted plot for regression
- Confusion matrix for classification
- Feature-importance chart
- Final action/recommendation slide

## Communication principles

Avoid unnecessary mathematical detail. Explain metrics in practical terms, disclose uncertainty and limitations, and connect each finding to an agricultural decision.
